## The decision being explained/What do they care

- Forest owner decide on the optimal cutting age.
- Forest owner's preferences toward risk, and toward ambiguity.
- Providinng recommendations to forest owners that take their preferences into account.

Research questions:

**"Does the optimal cutting age depend on the forest owner’s preferences towards risk
and ambiguity? What is the value of information that will make it possible to resolve each type of
ambiguity?"**

- Value of information

    - Calculated as the value that makes the forest owner indifferent to the risky situation without ambiguity and the situation with ambiguity.
