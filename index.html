Experimental · Environmental · Agricultural Economics

Quang Huy Nguyen

Economist based in Strasbourg, France

I study how information, incentives, and institutions shape sustainable technology adoption, food choices, and climate adaptation.

My work combines laboratory and field experiments, applied econometrics, evidence synthesis, and economic theory. I completed my PhD in Economics at the University of Strasbourg and conducted postdoctoral research at GAEL, Université Grenoble Alpes.

Currently seeking postdoctoral and research opportunities.
